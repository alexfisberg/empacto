Impact projects need to move without losing their roots.

Our logo is inspired by the Paxiúba, an Amazonian palm with exposed roots, known for moving in search of light without letting go of the ground. That is how we work: we help each initiative evolve with method, evidence and financial sustainability, without losing the purpose that gave rise to it.

  1. A solid foundation to scale

    Clear purpose, a well-defined strategy and a consistent structure so the initiative can grow and expand its impact.

  2. Field and evidence

    Grounded in field-tested best practices and clear evaluation processes for decision-making.

  3. Down-to-earth experience

    Our team has lived the day-to-day of organizations and projects. We roll up our sleeves alongside each team and build tailor-made solutions.

Consulting, Implementation, Mentoring and Evaluation.

We work together to understand what your initiative needs right now.

Strategy

  • 01Strategic planning

    We support the planning of impact projects and initiatives at different levels of maturity, always with a close eye on financial sustainability and governance.

  • 02Social investment management

    Portfolio building and management, curation of organizations and resource optimization for companies, foundations and donor families. Volunteer program management for companies.

  • 03Project and organization development

    From idea to implementation: design, budget, timeline and structure.

Resources and sustainability

  • 04Fundraising

    Income diversification and fundraising strategy for financial sustainability. Planning, development, management and reporting for tax-incentive projects (Lei Rouanet, Lei da Reciclagem, ProAc).

  • 05Sustainability strategies

    Assessment of best-practice opportunities, waste management implementation, composting projects and sourcing of sustainable materials.

  • 06B Corp Certification

    Assessment and development plan toward B Corp certification.

Evidence and learning

  • 07Research and evaluation

    In-depth research and building of impact measurement and evaluation processes.

  • 08Content and courses

    Well-grounded content production and tailor-made courses or talks.

  • 09Workshops and facilitation

    Design of thematic workshops and facilitation of gatherings for strategic teams.

A group of four friends mobilized their networks in a chain of emergency donations at the start of the pandemic. EmPacto curated the initiatives, considering the vulnerability of the populations served, the reliability of the organizations and the potential for impact.

  • Emergency food and basic hygiene supplies in different regions of Brazil
  • Protective equipment for doctors and health workers
  • Support for waste pickers, people experiencing homelessness, Indigenous peoples, quilombola communities, trans people and low-income urban communities
  • Two rounds of curation, with an average donation of R$ 1,400 per initiative
  • Funds managed by Instituto Sincronicidade, free of charge
